Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you call or visit.

What happens at the first consultation?

We listen, review any documents you bring and explain your options, likely steps and costs.

What should I bring?

Your national ID, any agreements, letters, court papers or titles related to the matter, and a list of questions.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the work involved. We explain them and put them in writing before we start.

Can you appear in court for me?

Yes. Our advocates represent clients in the High Court and the Magistrates’ Courts.

Which office should I visit?

Either. Both offices handle all practice areas, and we can transfer files between them.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. We treat all client information as confidential.
