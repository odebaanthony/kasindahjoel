Insights

Short guides in plain language. They are general information, not legal advice.

Land and property

Before you pay for land

Ask to see the title and the seller’s identity, search the land registry, visit the plot with local leaders, and have an advocate review the sale agreement before any money changes hands.

Family and succession

Why write a will

A valid will lets you decide who receives your property and who looks after your children, and it spares your family long disputes. An advocate can make sure it is properly drafted and witnessed.

Business

Starting a business in Uganda

Choose a structure such as a sole business or a company, register it, obtain the licences your sector needs and put key agreements in writing from day one.

Criminal

If you are arrested

You have the right to remain silent and to speak to an advocate. Stay calm, do not sign statements you do not understand and contact a lawyer or your family as soon as you can.
