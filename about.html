About Kasindha Law Firm

A Ugandan firm with a city office and a district office, built around the people we serve.

Our story

We opened our Kampala office to serve clients in the capital’s courts and businesses, and our Iganga office to bring the same standard of advice to Busoga and eastern Uganda, so people do not have to travel far for good counsel.

Today ten people work across both offices: advocates, an assistant legal officer and office assistants. Each file has someone who knows it and someone who answers the phone.

  • Plain languageWe explain the law and your options without jargon.
  • Honest adviceWe tell you when a case is strong, when it is not, and what it may cost.
  • ConfidentialityWhat you tell us stays between you and the firm.
  • Close to youTwo offices, so help is never far.

Our mission

To make good legal advice reachable for ordinary Ugandans and growing businesses, in the city and in the district.

Who we serve

Families, landowners, traders, employees, small and medium businesses, and organisations that need dependable legal support.
